# Node Research: Branches

An add-on for Node Research that trades the floating physics bubbles for a tidy tree growing from left to right, like the vanilla research screen.

- Foundations and starting projects sit on the left, and each branch fans out to the right from the research it builds on.
- Everything a project needs sits to its left; extra prerequisites are joined by their own lines.
- Earlier eras appear as a single era bubble at the left edge, and era advancement sits at the far right.
- Expanding or collapsing a bubble makes its branch make room, sliding the tree smoothly into place.
- Everything else is Node Research as usual: bubbles, eras, fog of war, the queue, search and the side panel.
